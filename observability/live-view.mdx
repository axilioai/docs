Live view streams a phone's screen to your
[dashboard](https://app.axilio.ai/dashboard) in real time, so you can watch your
agent work as it happens.

Open any active session in the dashboard to see its live view. It's the fastest
way to see what your agent is doing right now, and to catch a problem while it's
still happening.

The live view also exists outside the dashboard: every allocation returns a
`live_view_url` you can open directly or
[embed in your own app](/observability/embed-live-view) as an iframe.

To review a session after it ends, use a [recording](/observability/recordings)
instead.
