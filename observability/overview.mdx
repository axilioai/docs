Observability covers the phone's visual state during and after a session. Use
Live view to watch an active phone. Use Recordings to replay the screen after a
recorded session finishes processing.

## Choose the right surface

| You want to | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Watch or embed the phone screen while a session is active | [Live view](/observability/live-view) |
| Replay the screen after the session | [Recordings](/observability/recordings) |
| Understand operations, timing, output, errors, or per-call cost | [Telemetry](/telemetry/overview) |

Live view, recording, and Telemetry use different credentials and controls.
A Telemetry URL is read-only. A live-view URL can carry broader phone
privileges unless the session is view-only. Neither live URL is a recording or
archive credential.

## Follow a visual session

1. Open [**Sessions**](https://app.axilio.ai/dashboard/axilio/sessions).
2. Select an active session to watch its screen in **Live view**.
3. After the session ends, wait for its recording status to become `ready`.
4. Replay the recording and, when useful, compare it with the session's
   [Telemetry dashboard](/telemetry/understand-a-trace).

Recording playback can synchronize its playhead with Telemetry time so you can
compare screen changes with the operations that caused them.

## Control recording separately

Recording and Telemetry are enabled by default, but they are independent:

- `recording: false` prevents the screen recording and rolling thumbnail from
  being written.
- `telemetry: false` prevents both live and retained Telemetry without
  disabling recording.

See [Telemetry data controls](/telemetry/data-controls) for retention,
sensitive-data, and Telemetry opt-out guidance.
