Axilio gives each phone session three complementary views:

- **Live view** shows the phone screen while the session is active.
- **Telemetry** explains what ran through spans and logs, live or from the
  retained frame archive.
- **Recordings** replay the screen after a recorded session finishes processing.

Open [**Sessions**](https://app.axilio.ai/dashboard/axilio/sessions) and select a
session to see its screen, execution timeline, logs, and recording together.

## Follow one session from live to archived

1. Open **Live view** while the phone is active to watch the screen.
2. Follow **Live Telemetry** to see operations and logs as they arrive.
3. After the session ends, use **Frames** as the retained execution record.
4. When recording was enabled and its status is `ready`, replay the screen
   beside the timeline.

Live capability URLs expire with the session. Archived frames and recordings
follow the organization's retention policy and can become unavailable later.

## Supported interfaces

| Interface | Current observability surface |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Live screen, execution timeline, logs, recording playback, and per-call cost. |
| **CLI** | `axilio sessions trace`, `axilio sessions trace --follow`, and `axilio runs watch`. The CLI does not retrieve recordings. |
| **Python** | `client.telemetry(...).tail()`, `.trace()`, `.summary()`, and `.logs()`, plus `client.phones.session_recording(...)`. |
| **Go** | The `drivers/telemetry` package, plus `client.Phones.SessionRecording(...)`. |
| **REST** | Session frames, Telemetry-token, live-view-token, and recording operations. |

## Data controls

Recording and Telemetry are on by default, but they are independent controls.
The same Telemetry rule applies to direct allocations and workflow runs:

- `recording: false` prevents the screen recording and rolling thumbnail from
  being written.
- On a direct phone allocation or workflow run, `telemetry: false` prevents
  both live and archived Telemetry frames.

Your organization's data-retention policy can also prevent these records from
being created or can make an older recording or trace unavailable. See
[Recordings](/observability/recordings) and [Frames](/observability/frames) for
the states each interface returns.
