Embedding, authentication modes, view-only sessions, appearance, CSP, and
capability-URL safety are now documented together on [Live view](/observability/live-view).

The minimal embed is one iframe:

```html
<iframe
  src="{live_view_url}"
  allow="autoplay; clipboard-read; clipboard-write"
  style="width: 100%; height: 100%; border: 0;"
></iframe>
```
