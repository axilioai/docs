Axilio currently offers **dedicated phones** billed as flat rentals.

- Rent a phone at a **daily, weekly, or monthly** rate.
- Sessions on a dedicated phone add **\$0 in device-time charges**.
- Vision calls such as `find(query=...)` can incur separate usage charges.
- Everything is itemized, so you always know
  [where your money went](/observability/cost).

Your rental is billed through its subscription. Add funds to cover metered usage
such as paid vision calls.
