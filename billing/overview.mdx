Billing and usage are related but separate:

- [**Usage**](/usage/overview) shows phone sessions, aggregate spend, and
  individual inference charges.
- [**Balance, credits, and invoices**](/billing/balance-and-invoices) explains
  available credit, purchases, expiration, and payment records.
- **Current plan and plan changes** appear on the
  [**Billing**](https://app.axilio.ai/dashboard/axilio/billing) page. The current
  organization plan appears in the summary near the top. Click **Change Plan**
  to open the plan-selection modal. Its muted gray style does not mean it is
  disabled. There is no separate **Plans** page or tab in the dashboard.
- [**Dedicated phone pricing**](/billing/plans) shows the current daily, weekly,
  and monthly rental prices.

## Organization plans

Every organization is on one plan. Your plan sets the subscription price, the
usage credit it includes, the rate for shared-pool phone time, how many runs
can execute at once, and how long Axilio keeps logs and Telemetry:

<div id="axilio-org-plans" />

Each paid invoice adds your plan's included credit to your balance, so a
yearly subscription gives you twelve months of credit at once. On Hobby, you
get the credit once, when you sign up. Your Enterprise contract sets its own
terms, including retention.

The current plan appears on the dashboard's **Billing** page. The **Change
Plan** modal shows the options available to your organization without
navigating away from **Billing**. The page's tabs are **Dedicated Phones**,
**Invoices**, and **Preferences**.

## Shared-pool and dedicated phones

A session runs on one of two kinds of phone:

- **Shared pool**: phones from Axilio's fleet, available to every
  organization. You pay for session time per second, at your plan's rate (see
  the table above). When the session ends, Axilio deducts the charge from your
  balance at the rate of the plan the session started on.
- **Dedicated**: a phone your organization rents for a daily, weekly, or
  monthly period. You pay for the rental, and its sessions add no phone-time
  charge. The rental is separate from your organization plan. See
  [Dedicated phone pricing](/billing/plans).

If you allocate without a `phone_id` or a `pool`, you get a shared-pool phone.
To use a rented phone, pass `pool: "dedicated"` or the phone's `phone_id`. You
need a balance above zero to allocate a shared-pool phone. See
[Errors](/reference/errors) for what a low balance returns.

## What is billed

| Product | Billing model |
| --- | --- |
| Shared-pool phone | Per second of session time, at your plan's rate |
| Dedicated phone | Flat rental; its sessions add no phone-time charge |
| Vision inference | Metered per call according to the selected model |

Your balance can contain included credit and purchased credit. See
[How credits work](/billing/balance-and-invoices#how-credits-work) for spending
priority, renewal behavior, and expiration.
