A dedicated phone is billed at a flat daily, weekly, or monthly rate. It remains
reserved for your organization throughout the rental period, and its sessions add
no device-time charges.

<div id="axilio-rental-plans" />

These prices are pulled live from Axilio. See the
[pricing page](https://axilio.ai) for full details.

## Vision model pricing

`find(query=...)` and other vision calls use one of these models. Paid models are
billed separately from the phone's flat rental.

<div id="axilio-models" />

Prices are per million tokens, pulled live. See
[Usage and cost](/billing/usage-and-cost) to track what you actually spend.

## Rent or manage a phone

1. Open [**Phones**](https://app.axilio.ai/dashboard).
2. Click **Rent A Phone** and choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
3. Complete checkout.
4. To manage an existing rental, open **Settings → Billing → Dedicated Phones**.
