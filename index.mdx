Axilio gives agents **real, dedicated Android phones in the cloud**. Pick up a
phone, read its screen, then tap, type, and swipe just as a person would.

Use Axilio when a mobile app has no suitable public API. Your agent can work
through the same screen and carrier network a person uses, then turn a tested
interaction into a repeatable Python or Go program.

## Give your AI a phone

Paste this prompt into Claude, Codex, or Cursor. It installs the official CLI,
asks you to complete secure authentication, verifies a dedicated-phone session
with a read-only observation, and then helps you build a durable SDK script.
