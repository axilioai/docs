Axilio gives your agents **real Android phones in the cloud** and a simple Python
library to control them. Pick up a phone, read its screen, and tap-type-swipe, just like a person would.

Most apps don't expose a public API. So an agent has to
use them the same way you do: on a real phone. That's what Axilio is for.

## Give your AI a phone

Paste this into your AI coding agent (Cursor, Claude, Codex, Windsurf, or
similar). It installs the Axilio CLI, loads the agent instructions, guides
secure authentication, and verifies the setup in a live phone session before
building a reusable workflow.
